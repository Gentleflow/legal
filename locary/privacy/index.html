Privacy Policy

Locary · Effective August 22, 2026

In short: Locary needs no account, has no ads or behavioral analytics, and does not track you across apps. Your map and media stay on your device by default. If you enable Pro sync, Locary stores a backup in your own private iCloud database, which Gentleflow cannot view. Apple provides maps, iCloud, permissions, and payments; RevenueCat verifies Pro access and provides purchase analytics.

1. Who we are

Locary (“the App”) is developed by Gentleflow (“we”, “us”, “our”). Contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Information the App handles

  • Private map content: coordinates, place names, dates, text, notes, favorites, music details, and other metadata you enter or create.
  • Media: photos and videos you select or capture, and voice recordings you create. These are copied into the App's private local storage.
  • Photo-library footprints: if you choose photo import or Photo Sync, Locary reads asset identifiers, capture dates, and locations from the system photo library to group geotagged photos into visits. The original photos remain in the system library and are not copied or uploaded by this feature.
  • Location: when you tap the location control or create a place-based memory, Locary may use your current location after permission. MapKit and Apple's geocoding services may process coordinates to display the map and resolve a place name.
  • Optional iCloud sync: when a Pro user enables sync, Locary uploads a versioned snapshot of place and entry data, together with media already copied into Locary, to that user's private CloudKit database. Existing snapshots can be restored without Pro. Gentleflow does not receive or have access to this private database.
  • Subscription data: product, transaction, entitlement, country/region, and device/OS-related information handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive payment-card details or Apple Account credentials.
  • Technical data: when RevenueCat is contacted, it may receive ordinary service data such as a pseudonymous App User ID, IP address, device and OS information, request status, and service events.
  • Exports: when you choose backup export, Locary creates a ZIP containing your data and media. Apple's share sheet sends it only to the destination you choose.

3. How and why we use information

Locary uses this information to save and display your private memory map, attach content to places, search and review entries, import photo footprints, create backups you request, sync and restore your private iCloud snapshot, verify Pro access, restore purchases, troubleshoot failures, and meet legal obligations. We do not use IDFA, serve ads, sell personal information, or track activity across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Storage and service providers

  • On-device storage. Map content and Locary-managed media are stored in the App's private local container by default. Locary has no Gentleflow account or Gentleflow-operated memory server.
  • Your private iCloud database. If you enable Pro sync, Locary uses CloudKit container iCloud.tech.gentleflow.locary to store one current, versioned snapshot and its referenced media in your Apple Account's private database. The snapshot is published only after its media uploads complete. Gentleflow cannot browse or retrieve this private content.
  • Apple. Apple provides CloudKit/iCloud, MapKit, geocoding, system permissions, media pickers, App Store payments, sharing, and optional App Store diagnostics under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat. RevenueCat verifies and restores purchases and manages entitlements. It may process a pseudonymous App User ID plus transaction, product, country, device, OS, IP, and service-event information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions and your choices

  • Location, camera, microphone, photo selection, and Apple Music access are requested only when relevant to a feature you choose.
  • You can deny or later change permissions in iOS Settings. Core browsing and existing local memories remain available when optional permissions are denied.
  • The system photo picker limits manual attachment access to the items you choose. Photo-footprint import and Photo Sync request photo-library access so Locary can scan capture dates and locations; imported originals remain in the system library.
  • iCloud upload is off by default and requires Pro. Turning sync off stops future uploads but does not itself delete an existing private iCloud snapshot. Restoring an existing snapshot is always available without Pro; when local data is not empty, Locary asks before replacing it.
  • You choose every backup destination through Apple's share sheet.

6. Diagnostics

If “Share With App Developers” is enabled on your device, Apple may provide crash and performance reports through App Store Connect. Locary contains no independent analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

7. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete entries or delete the App. If sync is enabled, later uploads replace the current iCloud snapshot and remove media no longer referenced by that snapshot. Turning sync off or deleting the App does not by itself remove the existing private iCloud snapshot; you control iCloud data through your Apple Account and Apple's system settings. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or send them. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase and service records according to their policies and legal obligations. To request deletion of data, if any, that Gentleflow can reasonably associate with you, email us. Some transaction records cannot be deleted where law requires retention.

8. Children

Locary is not directed to children and does not ask users to create an account or provide contact details. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us so we can evaluate the request.

9. Security, rights, and changes

Locary uses Apple's application sandbox, private CloudKit database, and platform protections, but no system is completely secure. Apple and RevenueCat may process service data in other countries under their safeguards. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing. Locary content is held on your device and, only when sync is enabled, in your private iCloud database; both are controlled through your device and Apple Account. We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above.

10. Contact

Email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

隐私政策

迹忆 · 2026 年 8 月 22 日生效

一句话：迹忆无需账号，无广告、无行为分析，也不跨应用追踪。地图与媒体默认保存在你的设备上；开启 Pro 同步后，备份会进入你自己的 iCloud 私人数据库，Gentleflow 无法查看。Apple 提供地图、iCloud、系统权限与支付，RevenueCat 用于校验 Pro 权益并提供购买分析。

1. 我们是谁

迹忆（英文名 Locary，“本应用”）由 Gentleflow（“我们”）开发。如有隐私问题，请联系 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。

2. 本应用处理的信息

  • 私人地图内容：你输入或创建的坐标、地点名、日期、文字、备注、收藏状态、音乐信息及其他元数据。
  • 媒体：你主动选择或拍摄的照片和视频，以及主动录制的语音。这些内容会复制到应用的本机私有存储。
  • 相册足迹：当你使用照片导入或照片同步时，迹忆会从系统相册读取资源标识符、拍摄时间与地点，用于把带位置的照片整理成一次到访。此功能不会复制或上传系统相册中的原图。
  • 位置：当你点击定位或创建地点记忆时，迹忆会在取得授权后使用当前位置。MapKit 与 Apple 地理编码服务可能处理坐标，以显示地图并解析地点名称。
  • 可选 iCloud 同步：Pro 用户开启同步后，迹忆会把地点与记录数据的版本化快照，以及已经复制进迹忆的关联媒体，上传到该用户的 CloudKit 私人数据库。已有快照无需 Pro 也可以下载恢复。Gentleflow 不会接收、也无法访问该私人数据库。
  • 订阅数据：Apple 与 RevenueCat 处理的商品、交易、权益、国家/地区及设备/系统相关信息。我们不会收到银行卡详情或 Apple 账户凭据。
  • 技术数据：连接 RevenueCat 时，其可能收到化名 App User ID、IP 地址、设备与系统信息、请求状态及服务事件等常规服务数据。
  • 导出：当你选择备份导出时，迹忆会创建包含数据和媒体的 ZIP；仅由 Apple 分享面板发送到你选择的目标。

3. 使用目的

迹忆处理上述信息，用于保存和展示私人记忆地图、将内容附在地点、搜索和回看记录、导入照片足迹、生成你主动请求的备份、同步和恢复你的私人 iCloud 快照、验证 Pro 权益、恢复购买、排查故障及履行法律义务。我们不使用 IDFA、不投放广告、不出售个人信息，也不跨其他公司的应用或网站追踪你。

4. 存储与服务商

  • 本机存储。地图内容与由迹忆管理的媒体默认保存在应用的本机私有容器。迹忆不提供 Gentleflow 账号，也没有由 Gentleflow 运营的记忆服务器。
  • 你的 iCloud 私人数据库。开启 Pro 同步后，迹忆使用 CloudKit 容器 iCloud.tech.gentleflow.locary，在你的 Apple 账户私人数据库中保存一份当前的版本化快照及其关联媒体。所有媒体上传完成后才会发布该快照；Gentleflow 无法浏览或取回这些私人内容。
  • Apple。Apple 依据其隐私政策提供 CloudKit/iCloud、MapKit、地理编码、系统权限、媒体选择器、App Store 支付、分享及可选的 App Store 诊断。
  • RevenueCat。RevenueCat 用于校验和恢复购买、管理会员权益，可能处理化名 App User ID，以及交易、商品、国家/地区、设备、系统、IP 与服务事件信息。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

5. 权限与选择

  • 位置、相机、麦克风、照片选择与 Apple Music 访问只会在你主动使用相关功能时请求。
  • 你可以拒绝授权，或之后在 iOS 设置中更改。拒绝可选权限后，仍可浏览现有本机记忆。
  • 手动添加附件时，系统照片选择器将读取范围限制为你主动选择的项目。照片足迹导入与照片同步会请求相册权限，以扫描拍摄时间和地点；导入的原图仍留在系统相册。
  • iCloud 上传默认关闭且需要 Pro。关闭同步会停止后续上传，但不会自动删除已有的私人 iCloud 快照。下载恢复已有快照始终不需要 Pro；本机已有数据时，迹忆会先询问是否替换。
  • 每次备份的接收目标均由你通过 Apple 分享面板选择。

6. 诊断

若设备开启“与 App 开发者共享”，Apple 可能通过 App Store Connect 提供崩溃和性能报告。迹忆不含独立分析 SDK 或广告 SDK。

7. 留存与删除

本机内容会保留至你删除记录或删除应用。开启同步后，后续上传会替换当前 iCloud 快照，并移除该快照不再引用的媒体。关闭同步或删除应用不会自动移除已有的私人 iCloud 快照；你可通过 Apple 账户与系统设置管理 iCloud 数据。导出文件会保留在你选择保存或发送的位置。Apple 与 RevenueCat 按各自政策和法律义务留存购买与服务记录。如需删除 Gentleflow 能合理关联到你的数据（如有），请发送邮件；法律要求保存的部分交易记录可能无法删除。

8. 儿童隐私

迹忆不面向儿童，也不要求注册账号或提供联系方式。如你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们核实处理。

9. 安全、权利与变更

迹忆使用 Apple 应用沙盒、CloudKit 私人数据库及平台保护，但任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。Apple 与 RevenueCat 可能在其他国家处理服务数据。根据你所在地法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理的权利。迹忆内容保存在你的设备上；仅在开启同步后，才会同时保存在你的 iCloud 私人数据库中，两者均由你的设备和 Apple 账户控制。我们可能更新本政策，并修改上方生效日期。
