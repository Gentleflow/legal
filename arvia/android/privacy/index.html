Privacy Policy

Arvia for Android

Effective November 10, 2026. Arvia is developed by Gentleflow. Contact: gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address or phone number to play. Arvia has no advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not run our own gameplay analytics. We do not request contacts, photos, location, microphone access or your browsing history.

Local game data

Your level progress, saved boards, hint allowance, statistics, daily challenge records, Hell records and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Puzzles work offline. We do not upload your moves to our servers. You can export a progress backup file to a location you choose with the Android file picker; the file stays under your control.

Optional local reminders

Daily Challenge reminders are optional and off by default. If you enable them and allow notifications, Arvia schedules local notifications on your device using your chosen time and locally stored challenge completion records. These reminders do not send your play history to a server or use a remote push token. Completing today’s challenge cancels its pending reminder. You can turn reminders off in Arvia or manage notification permission in Android Settings. Android may delay delivery to save battery.

Local progress storage

Arvia saves game progress on this device and disables Android app-data cloud backup and device transfer. It does not provide cloud saves or cross-device progress synchronization.

Google Play Games leaderboards

Hell mode uses Google Play Games Services for its leaderboards. Google may sign you in automatically according to your Play Games settings. When signed in, Hell scores are submitted with the reached floor, best combo, a flawless-run indicator and a country/region code derived from the device locale (not GPS location). Google associates these with your game-specific player identity and shows rank, display name, score and region subject to your Play Games profile visibility. The friends board uses Play Games friend identities only after platform authorization; it does not read your address book. Pending scores are stored locally for offline retry. Google also processes Play Games SDK usage analytics and diagnostics for reliability and product improvement. We do not use leaderboard data for advertising. Manage sign-in, visibility and friends access in Play Games settings; delete game data through your Play Games profile or Google Account controls. Campaign, daily and Hell play remain available without Play Games.

Premium purchases

Google Play processes optional subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase. We do not receive your card or bank details. RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage Premium, and provides purchase and subscription reporting. It handles an app-specific customer identifier, purchase records and tokens, subscription status, and technical information needed to operate the service, such as platform, app version, locale and currency. Purchase-service connections use encryption in transit. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name or email or enable advertising attribution integrations. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play age signals

In supported regions, Arvia requests access to age-range and approval signals provided by Google Play under your or your parent’s sharing settings. Google Play manages any sharing or verification prompt. Arvia processes these signals only in memory, without saving or logging them or sending them to RevenueCat. They are not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or analytics. Declining optional sharing does not prevent free puzzle play. If Google Play requires age verification or parental approval, new purchases wait until that status is resolved. Restore purchases remains available.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending unrelated sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Clear Arvia’s storage in Android Settings to remove local game data. Uninstalling removes local game data. For purchase-service data, email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with enough information to locate your purchase, such as its Google Play order reference. We may ask for verification before acting. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, dispute resolution or other applicable obligations. Google controls its own store records. Deleting app or purchase-service data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in Google Play.

Children and changes

Arvia has no social features or advertising and does not ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. We will update this policy if the app’s data handling changes and show the updated effective date.

隐私政策

箭途 安卓版

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日。箭途 由 Gentleflow 开发。联系邮箱：gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。

无需注册即可游玩

你无需创建账户，也无需提供姓名、邮箱或电话号码即可游玩。箭途 没有广告或跨应用追踪，我们也不运行自己的游戏行为分析。我们不申请通讯录、照片、位置、麦克风或浏览历史的访问权限。

本地游戏数据

关卡进度、棋盘存档、提示次数、统计、每日挑战记录、炼狱记录和偏好设置保存在应用的私有存储中。解题可离线进行，我们不会将你的操作上传到自己的服务器。你可以通过安卓文件选择器把进度备份文件导出到自己选择的位置，该文件由你自行保管。

可选本地提醒

每日挑战提醒为可选功能，默认关闭。开启提醒并允许通知后，箭途 会根据你选择的时间和本地挑战完成记录，在设备上安排本地通知。此功能不会将游玩记录发送到服务器，也不使用远程推送令牌。完成当天挑战后，会取消当天待发送的提醒。你可以在 箭途 内关闭提醒，或在安卓设置中管理通知权限。安卓可能为节省电量而延后送达。

本地进度存储

箭途 将游戏进度保存在当前设备，并禁用应用数据的安卓云备份和设备迁移。应用不提供云存档或跨设备进度同步。

Google Play 游戏排行榜

炼狱模式的排行榜使用 Google Play 游戏服务。Google 可能会根据你的 Play 游戏设置自动登录。登录后，炼狱成绩会连同到达楼层、最高连击、无失误标记以及由设备语言地区推断的国家/地区代码（非 GPS 位置）一起提交。Google 会将这些信息关联到你在本游戏中的玩家身份，并按你的 Play 游戏资料可见性展示排名、显示名称、分数和地区。好友榜仅在平台授权后使用 Play 游戏好友身份，不读取通讯录。待提交的成绩会保存在本地以便离线重试。Google 还会处理 Play 游戏 SDK 的使用统计和诊断数据，用于保障可靠性和改进产品。我们不会将排行榜数据用于广告。你可以在 Play 游戏设置中管理登录、可见性和好友权限，并通过 Play 游戏资料或 Google 账户删除游戏数据。不连接 Play 游戏也可以正常游玩主线、每日挑战和炼狱模式。

高级会员购买

可选订阅和永久会员由 Google Play 处理付款，我们不会获得你的银行卡或银行账户信息。RevenueCat 协助验证购买、恢复权益、管理会员，并提供购买及订阅报表。它处理应用专属客户标识、购买记录和令牌、订阅状态，以及提供服务所需的平台、应用版本、语言地区和货币等技术信息。购买服务连接在传输过程中加密。我们不向 RevenueCat 提供你的姓名或邮箱，也不启用广告归因集成。相关政策：https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy 和 https://policies.google.com/privacy。

Google Play 年龄信号

在支持的地区，箭途 会根据你或家长的共享设置，请求 Google Play 提供的年龄范围与批准状态。共享或验证提示由 Google Play 管理。应用仅在内存中处理这些信号，不保存、不记录日志、不发送给 RevenueCat，也不用于广告、营销、画像或分析。拒绝可选共享不会阻止免费解题。如果 Google Play 要求年龄验证或家长批准，新的购买会等待该状态解决；恢复购买仍然可用。

客户支持

你发送邮件时，我们会收到你自愿提供的信息，并用于处理你的请求。请勿发送与问题无关的敏感信息。

保留与删除

在安卓设置中清除 箭途 的存储可删除本地游戏数据。卸载会删除本地游戏数据。若要申请删除购买服务数据，请向 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com 发送邮件，并提供足以定位购买的信息，例如 Google Play 订单编号。执行前我们可能要求验证。部分记录可能因会计、争议处理或其他适用义务而保留。Google 自行管理其商店记录。删除应用或购买服务数据不会取消订阅，请在 Google Play 中另行取消。

儿童与政策更新

箭途 没有社交功能或广告，不要求儿童提交个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们发送了个人信息，请联系我们处理。数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策并注明新的生效日期。
