Privacy Policy

Kudoku for Android

Effective September 24, 2026. Kudoku is developed by Gentleflow. Contact: gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address or phone number to play. Kudoku has no advertising, cross-app tracking or gameplay analytics. We do not request contacts, photos, location, microphone access or your browsing history.

Local game data

Your progress, puzzle entries, notes, statistics, daily challenge records and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Puzzle play works offline. We do not upload your puzzle moves to our servers.

Optional local reminders

Daily Challenge reminders are optional and off by default. If you enable them and allow notifications, Kudoku schedules local notifications on your device using your chosen time and locally stored challenge completion records. These reminders do not send your play history to a server or use a remote push token. Completing today’s challenge cancels its pending reminder. You can turn reminders off in Kudoku or manage notification permission in Android Settings. Android may delay delivery to save battery.

Local progress storage

Kudoku saves game progress on this device and disables Android app-data cloud backup and device transfer. It does not provide cloud saves or cross-device progress synchronization. Existing backups made by older versions are managed through your device and backup provider.

Premium purchases

Google Play processes optional subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase. We do not receive your card or bank details. RevenueCat helps validate purchases, restore access and manage Premium, and provides purchase and subscription reporting. It handles an app-specific customer identifier, purchase records and tokens, subscription status, and technical information needed to operate the service, such as platform, app version, locale and currency. Purchase-service connections use encryption in transit. We do not provide RevenueCat with your name or email or enable advertising attribution integrations. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending unrelated sensitive information.

Retention and deletion

Reset progress in Kudoku deletes local play records and saved games while keeping settings, onboarding and Premium access. Clear Kudoku’s storage in Android Settings to remove local game data. Uninstalling removes local game data. For purchase-service data, email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with enough information to locate your purchase, such as its Google Play order reference. We may ask for verification before acting. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, dispute resolution or other applicable obligations. Google controls its own store records. Deleting app or purchase-service data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it separately in Google Play.

Children and changes

Kudoku has no social features or advertising and does not ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. We will update this policy if the app’s data handling changes and show the updated effective date.

本地进度存储

九重杀手数独 将游戏进度保存在当前设备，并禁用应用数据的安卓云备份和设备迁移。应用不提供云存档或跨设备进度同步。旧版本已经生成的备份由设备和备份服务提供商管理。
