Playing / 游戏说明

Each row, column and 3×3 box uses 1–9 once. Each cage adds up to its target without repeated digits. Play 25,000 campaign puzzles, daily challenges and Hell mode. No account or ads; puzzles work offline.

每行、每列和每个 3×3 宫都使用不重复的 1–9；每个笼子内数字不重复，并相加得到目标和。体验 25,000 道主线谜题、每日挑战和地狱模式。无需注册，没有广告，谜题支持离线游玩。

Purchases / 购买

Optional Premium offers monthly, yearly and lifetime plans. Use Restore purchases with the original Google Play account. To cancel, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kudoku. Uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions.

可选会员包含月付、年付和永久版。恢复购买时请使用原购买的 Google Play 账户。取消订阅请打开 Google Play → 付款和订阅 → 订阅 → Kudoku。卸载不会取消订阅。

Progress / 进度

Progress is saved only on this device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local progress.
进度仅保存在当前设备。清除应用存储或卸载会删除本地进度。
