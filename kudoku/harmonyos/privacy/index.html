隐私政策

九重杀手数独 · HarmonyOS 版本
更新日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日

九重杀手数独由鲁亮（Gentleflow）开发。本政策说明当前鸿蒙版本实际处理的数据。游戏无需注册账号，题库在设备本地运行。

我们保存哪些数据

游戏会在应用私有存储中保存当前棋盘、笔记、关卡进度、每日挑战记录、用时、失误数、地狱模式成绩及外观和辅助设置。这些数据用于恢复游戏、展示统计和记住您的选择。

设备权限与第三方服务

当前版本不申请通讯录、位置、相册、麦克风或摄像头权限，不包含广告、第三方统计 SDK、跨应用追踪或开发者自建账号。可选排行榜、会员云空间同步、内购、恢复购买、管理订阅、群星计划及在线政策页面需要联网，具体处理如下。iOS 版本的 Apple、iCloud 和 RevenueCat 服务不适用于本鸿蒙版本。

华为应用内支付

您可选择通过华为应用内支付（Huawei IAP）购买月付、年付订阅或永久会员。购买需要登录华为账号，华为负责账号身份、付款及订单处理，具体信息以华为支付页面及其隐私说明为准。我们不获取您的华为账号密码、银行卡号或支付密码。应用接收商品、价格、试用资格、签名订单及订阅状态，用于核验购买、授予或恢复会员权益，并在设备上保存会员商品标识及有效状态。购买记录不用于广告或用户画像。

订单服务与第三方处理

华为会向我们的订单服务发送购买、续费、取消、到期或退款等签名通知。服务处理商品标识、订单标识、购买令牌、事件类型、时间、订阅状态以及查询得到的金额和币种，用于核验订单事件、查询金额、去重及处理支付支持问题。订单通知服务托管于 Cloudflare；开发者通过 Bark 接收仅含应用、商品、交易状态及金额的运营提醒，不在提醒正文中包含购买令牌或订单号。这些服务可能在中国境外处理相关订单信息，游戏棋盘、笔记及进度不会因此上传。上述服务的用途限于订单服务与运营支持。您不购买也可以使用免费游戏功能。

可选炼狱排行榜

排行榜可以直接浏览。只有您主动点击“加入”，应用才会使用华为账号服务请求身份凭证。未登录时由华为系统显示登录界面，您可以取消。我们不获取华为账号密码、手机号或支付密码。

我们的云函数验证身份凭证，并将账号标识通过项目专属密钥派生成稳定的玩家编号，用于让同一华为账号在本应用内保留排行榜身份。身份凭证用于本次验证，不写入排行榜数据库。排行榜数据库保存派生玩家编号、随机词组名字的编号、成绩、到达层数、最高连击、无失误标记、设备系统设置提供的两位地区码、榜单所属周及更新时间。我们不读取精确位置，也不上传游戏棋盘或笔记。

排行榜使用华为 Account Kit、AppGallery Connect 云函数与云数据库，数据处理位置为中国。其他玩家可查看排行榜上展示的名字、名次、分数、层数、连击、地区和无失误标记。名字由应用提供的固定词表组合，不支持自由文字。

加入时已有的最佳成绩只补交总榜；加入后的成绩在过层或结束时提交到总榜及当周榜。应用在本机保存玩家编号、名字编号和周榜缓存。重置游戏进度不会自动删除已上传的排行榜记录。清除应用数据或卸载后，在您再次主动加入前本应用不再上传排行榜成绩；这也不会自动删除已经上传的记录。

周榜保留约八周，过期记录在后续成绩提交触发的清理中分批删除；因此删除时间可能晚于八周。总榜记录和名字在功能运营期间保存。如需查询、更正或删除已上传记录，请联系 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com，并提供榜上显示的名字以便核实。请勿发送账号密码、身份凭证或支付信息。

华为云空间同步

在支持的设备上，会员可在系统华为云空间中启用本应用同步。此时进度、每日挑战、统计及偏好通过华为端云同步服务在同一华为账号设备间同步；排行榜身份缓存独立保存在本机，不随游戏进度同步。关闭系统同步后不再同步新进度，云端副本由华为云空间设置管理。开发者不能读取您个人云空间中的进度。

群星计划

只有主动打开群星计划入口后，应用才会向 appunion.chiyoushu.com 请求推荐内容，并发送本机随机标识、harmonyos 平台标识、被展示或点击的应用编号，用于展示和点击去重。该标识不与华为账号关联，清除应用数据或卸载会清除本机标识；不会发送游戏进度或购买记录。

振动反馈

游戏使用设备振动提供操作反馈，可在应用设置中关闭。当前版本不发送通知，也不申请通知授权。上述功能不上传游戏数据。

首次使用选择

在您同意本政策和使用条款之前，应用不会加载游戏存档或音频。应用单独在本地记录您接受的政策版本，用于避免重复提示。您可选择不同意并退出；清除应用数据会删除同意记录和游戏进度。

保留与删除

除已启用的云空间同步及主动上传的排行榜记录外，本机游戏数据保留在设备上，直至您在系统设置中清除应用数据或卸载应用。清除后无法通过开发者服务器恢复。操作系统层面的备份、恢复及诊断由设备系统设置及华为相应政策管理。

订单通知队列在金额查询结束后清除查询令牌，在提醒投递成功后清除提醒正文；已完成事件的去重及运行记录在接收超过 35 天后清理。未完成事件保留至处理完成。必要的交易支持记录按处理问题的需要保留；华为持有的交易记录由其规则管理。卸载应用或清除本地数据不会取消订阅或删除华为订单。您可以通过下方邮箱提出数据查询、更正或删除请求；请勿提供支付密码或完整银行卡信息。

儿童与未成年人

游戏本身不要求玩家提供姓名、年龄、电话或身份信息。华为支付流程可能按其规则要求身份或付款信息。未成年人应在监护人的指导下使用，购买前取得监护人同意；请合理安排游戏时间。

政策更新

如数据处理方式发生变化，我们会在相关功能启用前更新本政策，并按适用要求提供相应说明和选择。

Privacy policy

Updated September 28, 2026

Kudoku for HarmonyOS stores puzzles, notes, progress, daily challenges, statistics, Hell runs and preferences in the app's private local storage. Gameplay requires no developer account. There are no ads, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. Optional leaderboard, Huawei Cloud synchronization, purchase services and App Union use the network as described below.

Purchases and services

Optional monthly and yearly subscriptions and lifetime Premium use Huawei IAP and require a HUAWEI ID and a network connection. Huawei handles account authentication, payment and orders under the notices shown in its payment flow. We do not receive your account password, card number or payment password. The app receives product information, prices, trial eligibility, signed orders and subscription status to verify, grant and restore Premium, and stores the membership product identifier and entitlement status locally. Purchase data is not used for advertising or profiling. Apple, iCloud and RevenueCat services used by the iOS version do not apply here.

Huawei sends signed purchase, renewal, cancellation, expiry and refund notifications to our order service hosted on Cloudflare. It processes product and order identifiers, purchase tokens, event types, times, subscription status, and queried amounts and currencies for order verification, amount lookup, deduplication and payment support. Bark delivers operational alerts to the developer containing only the app, product, transaction status and amount; alert bodies contain no order number or purchase token. These services may process order information outside mainland China. Puzzles, notes and progress are not sent to them. Free gameplay remains available without purchasing.

Optional Hell leaderboard

You can browse the leaderboard without joining. Only after you select Join does the app request an identity token through HUAWEI Account Kit. Huawei may show its sign-in screen if needed; you can cancel. We do not receive your HUAWEI ID password, phone number, or payment password.

Our cloud function verifies the token and derives a stable player identifier from the account identifier using a key specific to this app. The token is used for that verification and is not stored in the leaderboard database. Stored leaderboard data includes the derived player identifier, a name represented by fixed word-list indices, score, floor reached, best combo, flawless flag, the two-letter region code from system settings, board week, and update time. We do not access precise location or upload puzzle boards or notes for this feature.

The feature uses Huawei Account Kit and AppGallery Connect Cloud Functions and Cloud DB, with processing in China. Other players can see the displayed name, rank, score, floor, combo, region, and flawless marker. Names are combinations from fixed word lists; free text is not accepted.

When you join, your existing best score is submitted only to the all-time board. Subsequent scores are submitted to both the current-week and all-time boards at stage completion or the end of a run. The app stores your player identifier, name indices, and weekly cache locally. Resetting game progress does not delete uploaded leaderboard records. After clearing app data or uninstalling, leaderboard uploads stop until you explicitly join again; existing server records are not automatically deleted.

Weekly records are retained for approximately eight weeks and then removed in batches when later score submissions trigger cleanup, so deletion may take longer. All-time records and names remain while the feature operates. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with the name shown on the leaderboard so we can verify the request. Do not send passwords, identity tokens, or payment details.

Huawei Cloud sync

On supported devices, Premium members can enable app synchronization in Huawei Cloud settings. Progress, daily records, statistics and preferences then synchronize between devices using the same HUAWEI ID through Huawei cloud synchronization services. Leaderboard identity caches stay local and are separate from game-progress synchronization. Turning synchronization off stops new progress syncing; manage cloud copies in Huawei Cloud settings. The developer cannot read progress in your personal Huawei Cloud space.

App Union

Only opening the App Union entry sends requests to appunion.chiyoushu.com. Requests contain a local random identifier, the harmonyos platform tag and the displayed or clicked app ID for impression/click deduplication. The identifier is not linked to your HUAWEI ID and its local copy is removed when app data is cleared or uninstalled. Game progress and purchase records are not sent to this service.

Retention and choices

The notification queue clears lookup tokens after the amount lookup ends and clears alert content after successful delivery. Completed-event deduplication and operational records are cleaned up after 35 days from receipt; unfinished events remain until processed. Necessary transaction support records are retained as needed to resolve issues. Huawei retains its own transaction records under its rules. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; do not send payment passwords or full card details.

The app requests no contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera permission. It sends no notifications and requests no notification permission. Vibration can be disabled in settings. The accepted policy version is stored locally; gameplay and audio do not load before acceptance. Local game data remains until erased in system settings or by uninstalling. Erased progress cannot be recovered from our servers. Removing the app does not cancel subscriptions or erase Huawei orders. Device backup and diagnostics follow system settings and Huawei policies.

Minors and updates

Gameplay does not ask for names, ages, phone numbers or identity details. Huawei payment may require information under its own rules. Minors should use the app with a guardian's guidance and obtain permission before purchasing. We will update this policy before enabling features that change these practices.
