Privacy Policy

Sweeple · Effective July 22, 2026

In short: Sweeple needs no account and has no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Game data is stored on your device and may sync through your personal iCloud account when enabled. Apple and RevenueCat process purchases and subscriptions.

1. Who we are

Sweeple (the “App”) is developed by Gentleflow (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what data the App handles and how. Questions may be sent to gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

  • You do not need to create an account or provide a name, email address, phone number, or postal address to play.
  • The App does not access your contacts, photos, precise location, or browsing history.
  • We do not use an advertising identifier, serve ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal data or use it to build an advertising profile.

3. Game data stored by the App

  • On-device data. The App stores game progress, completed levels, gameplay statistics, streaks, brain-profile results, Hell Mode results, hint usage, and preferences in its private storage on your device.
  • iCloud sync (optional). When iCloud Sync is enabled, supported game data may be read from and written to Apple iCloud Key-Value Storage through your personal iCloud account. Publishing new progress to iCloud requires Premium; existing supported cloud data may still be restored when sync is enabled. This data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not operate a server that receives this game data.
  • Local notifications. If you enable reminders, the App schedules them on your device. Sweeple does not use its own push-notification server.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Sweeple offers optional Premium monthly and yearly subscriptions and a one-time Lifetime purchase. Apple processes payment through the App Store; we do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account credentials. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to load products, validate purchases, restore purchases, and manage Premium entitlement. RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID and transaction, subscription, device, operating-system, country or region, IP, and related technical information for those purposes. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics or diagnostics with app developers in your Apple device settings, Apple may make crash, performance, and related diagnostic information available to us through App Store Connect. Apple controls that collection and sharing under its privacy policy. Sweeple does not include a separate third-party analytics service.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. It contains no advertising or behavioral profiling.

7. Your choices

  • Control Sweeple’s iCloud sync in the App and your device’s iCloud settings.
  • Control reminders in Sweeple or in iOS Settings › Notifications › Sweeple.
  • Delete supported progress data using the controls provided in the App, or remove all local App data by deleting the App.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • For questions about purchase data processed for us, contact us at the address below.

8. Data retention

On-device and iCloud game data remain until you delete or replace them using the App or Apple’s controls. Apple and RevenueCat retain purchase and transaction information according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

9. International processing

Apple and RevenueCat may process information in countries or regions other than your own, subject to their respective safeguards and policies.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the App or applicable requirements change. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

隐私政策

Sweeple · 2026 年 7 月 22 日生效

一句话：Sweeple 无需注册账号，不含广告、第三方分析或跨应用追踪。游戏数据保存在你的设备上； 开启后，可通过你的个人 iCloud 账户同步。购买和订阅由 Apple 与 RevenueCat 处理。

1. 我们是谁

Sweeple（“本应用”）由 Gentleflow（“我们”）开发。本政策说明本应用处理哪些数据以及处理方式。 如有疑问，请联系 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。

2. 我们不会收集的信息

  • 游玩无需注册账号，也无需提供姓名、邮箱、电话号码或邮寄地址。
  • 本应用不会访问你的通讯录、照片、精确位置或浏览记录。
  • 我们不使用广告标识符，不展示广告，也不会跨其他公司的应用或网站追踪你。
  • 我们不会出售你的个人数据，也不会使用这些数据建立广告画像。

3. 本应用保存的游戏数据

  • 本机数据。本应用会在设备的应用私有存储中保存游戏进度、已完成关卡、游戏统计、 连胜、大脑档案结果、Hell Mode 成绩、提示使用情况和偏好设置。
  • iCloud 同步（可选）。开启“iCloud 同步”后，本应用可通过你的个人 iCloud 账户， 从 Apple iCloud 键值存储读取或写入支持的游戏数据。将新进度发布到 iCloud 需要 Premium；开启同步后， 仍可恢复已有的受支持云端数据。相关数据受 Apple 隐私政策约束。我们不运营接收这些游戏数据的服务器。
  • 本地通知。若你开启提醒，本应用会在设备本地安排通知。Sweeple 不使用自有推送通知服务器。

4. 购买与订阅

Sweeple 提供可选的 Premium 月订阅、年订阅和一次性永久购买。付款由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理； 我们不会收到你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账户凭据。我们使用 RevenueCat, Inc. 加载商品、 校验购买、恢复购买及管理 Premium 权益。为实现这些功能，RevenueCat 可能处理匿名应用用户标识以及交易、订阅、 设备、操作系统、国家或地区、IP 和相关技术信息。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

5. 诊断数据

如果你在 Apple 设备设置中选择与应用开发者共享分析或诊断数据，Apple 可能通过 App Store Connect 向我们提供崩溃、性能及相关诊断信息。相关收集与共享由 Apple 按其隐私政策控制。Sweeple 未集成独立的第三方分析服务。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用面向一般用户，不会在知情情况下要求儿童提供个人信息，也不含广告或行为画像。

7. 你的选择

  • 可在 Sweeple 内及设备的 iCloud 设置中控制同步。
  • 可在 Sweeple 内或 iOS 设置 › 通知 › Sweeple 中控制提醒。
  • 可使用应用内提供的功能删除支持的进度数据，或通过删除本应用清除全部本机应用数据。
  • 可在 iOS 设置 ›〔你的名字〕› 订阅 中管理或取消订阅。
  • 如对我们委托处理的购买数据有疑问，请通过下方地址联系我们。

8. 数据留存

本机与 iCloud 游戏数据会一直保留，直到你使用本应用或 Apple 提供的控制方式删除或替换它们。 Apple 与 RevenueCat 会根据各自政策和法律义务留存购买及交易信息。

9. 跨境处理

Apple 与 RevenueCat 可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理信息，并受其各自的保障措施和政策约束。

10. 政策变更

我们可能因应用功能或适用要求变化而更新本政策。重大变更会在此处通过新的生效日期体现。

11. 联系我们

对本政策有疑问？请发送邮件至 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。
