Common questions / 常见问题

How do I restore my purchase? / 如何恢复购买？

Open Sweeple, go to Settings, and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
打开 Sweeple，进入“设置”并点击“恢复购买”，同时确保登录的是购买时使用的 Apple 账户。

How do I manage or cancel a subscription? / 如何管理或取消订阅？

Open iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Sweeple. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription. The Lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
打开 iOS 设置 ›〔你的名字〕› 订阅 › Sweeple。删除应用不会取消仍在生效的订阅。永久版是一次性购买，不会续期。

Does my progress sync between devices? / 进度会在设备间同步吗？

Sweeple can sync supported game data through your personal iCloud account when iCloud Sync is enabled. Uploading new progress to iCloud is a Premium feature.
开启“iCloud 同步”后，Sweeple 可通过你的个人 iCloud 账户同步支持的游戏数据；将新进度上传至 iCloud 属于 Premium 功能。
