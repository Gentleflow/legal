Privacy Policy — Ninefold for Android

Effective and updated: September 13, 2026 · Package: tech.gentleflow.Sudoku

Ninefold is a Sudoku game with optional purchases. No account is required. We do not display ads or use advertising identifiers. Game progress stays on your device; Google Play and RevenueCat process purchase information to provide Premium.

Who operates Ninefold

Ninefold is developed and operated by Liang Lu (鲁亮), under the Gentleflow brand. Contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com for privacy questions or data requests.

Game data on your device

Puzzles, notes, progress, completion history, statistics, achievements and settings are stored in the app’s private storage. Ninefold does not upload this gameplay data to a developer-operated cloud service. Android may include eligible app data in system backup or device transfer if you enable those features. These are controlled by your device settings and the platform’s policies.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Google Play handles payment, subscriptions and refunds. We do not receive your card number, payment password or Google account credentials. Ninefold uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore access, maintain Premium entitlements and analyze subscription performance. This involves purchase history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase tokens, subscription status and dates, store/currency information, an app-generated anonymous customer identifier, and technical information such as app/SDK version and operating system needed to provide the service. Purchase information is processed even though no Ninefold account is required.

RevenueCat acts as our service provider. Data is transmitted using encryption in transit and may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. We do not sell personal data, use purchases for targeted advertising or collect advertising identifiers. We do not integrate a separate advertising, behavioral analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. Google may separately process data to operate Google Play, protect transactions and provide platform services.

Optional actions and permissions

  • Internet access supports store products, purchase verification, restoration and external links.
  • Notifications, if enabled, are scheduled locally. You can turn them off in Android settings.
  • Vibration follows your preferences. Sharing a result sends the poster to the app you choose through Android’s share sheet.
  • If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to respond. External websites and apps apply their own policies.

Retention, deletion and your choices

You can reset gameplay in the app or clear app data in Android settings to remove local information. Uninstalling removes local app storage; system backups are managed separately in your device/account settings. To request access, correction or deletion of purchase-related data held on our behalf by RevenueCat, email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com. Include enough information to locate your purchase, such as a Google Play order number; never send payment passwords or full card details. We may verify your request and retain records required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet these obligations.

Deleting app data or requesting data deletion does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. A later purchase or restore may recreate information necessary to validate your entitlement. Rights and response requirements depend on your location; you may contact your local data protection authority.

Children and policy updates

Ninefold is a general-audience puzzle game and does not request children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it. We will update this page when our practices change and show the effective date above.
