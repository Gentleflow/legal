Common questions / 常见问题

How do I restore my purchase? / 如何恢复购买？

Open the App, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
打开应用，进入"设置"点击"恢复购买"，并使用当初购买的同一 Apple ID 登录。

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? / 如何管理或取消订阅？

iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Ninefold. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
iOS 设置 ›〔你的名字〕› 订阅 › Ninefold。删除应用不会取消订阅。

Does my progress sync between devices? / 进度会在设备间同步吗？

Yes, privately through your own iCloud account when iCloud is enabled.
会的，在启用 iCloud 时，通过你自己的 iCloud 账户私密同步。
