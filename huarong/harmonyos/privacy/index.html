隐私政策（HarmonyOS 版）

一格华容道 · 生效及更新日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日

摘要：主要游戏功能可离线使用，无需注册本应用账号。可选购买、云空间同步、排行榜和群星计划使用网络。只有主动加入排行榜后，才上传派生玩家标识、随机昵称编号及成绩；不接入广告、分析或跨应用追踪 SDK。

1. 个人信息处理者

一格华容道 HarmonyOS 版（下称“本应用”）由鲁亮（开发者品牌：Gentleflow）开发和运营， 是本政策所述处理活动的个人信息处理者。隐私问题、投诉或权利请求可发送至 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。 我们通常会在 15 个工作日内答复。

2. 适用范围

本政策仅适用于从华为应用市场或其他合法渠道取得的“一格华容道”HarmonyOS 应用， 包名为 tech.gentleflow.huarong。其他平台或其他应用不适用本政策。

3. 本应用如何处理数据

主要游戏功能无需注册本应用账号。联网用途包括华为购买服务、可选排行榜、云空间同步和群星计划，具体如下。购买、恢复权益或管理订阅时，华为应用市场可能要求登录华为账号；本应用不会取得你的账号密码或支付凭据，也不读取设备标识符、通讯录、相册、 精确位置、剪贴板、相机或麦克风。

为实现单机游戏功能，本应用会在你的设备本地处理以下功能数据：

  • 游戏进度与统计：已完成关卡、最佳用时、完成次数、地狱模式进度、提示使用情况、游玩日期和脑力档案；用于保存进度、展示统计和继续游戏。
  • 进行中的游戏：数字棋盘排列、计时、移动次数和提示路径；用于恢复未完成的对局。
  • 应用偏好：主题、声音、音乐、振动及辅助显示开关；用于按照你的选择提供功能。
  • 购买与会员状态：为核验和恢复权益，本应用通过华为 IAP Kit 查询商品、订单和订阅状态，在设备上验证签名及应用归属；本地保存商品标识、购买时间、到期时间、核验时间和会员状态。订单令牌用于向华为确认交付，不上传至 Gentleflow 服务器。
  • 同意记录：已同意的政策版本；用于避免每次启动重复询问。该记录仅保存在本机。

本机功能数据保存在应用私有存储中。主动启用云空间同步或加入排行榜时，按下文说明处理相应数据；游戏进度不会发送给群星计划。

4. 设备权限与系统能力

  • 振动权限（ohos.permission.VIBRATE）：用于按你的设置提供按键和游戏结果触感反馈。你可在应用“设置”中关闭振动。
  • 网络权限（ohos.permission.INTERNET、ohos.permission.GET_NETWORK_INFO）： 用于华为购买服务、可选排行榜与云空间同步，以及第 5 节所述的“群星计划”应用互推：在你点开该入口后获取推荐应用列表、图标和详情，并上报展示与点击统计。 游戏本体（关卡、每日挑战、炼狱、统计与设置）完全离线，不联网也可正常游玩。 点击隐私政策、用户协议或联系我们时，本应用会请求 HarmonyOS 交由系统浏览器或邮件应用打开相应地址； 后续网络访问由你选择的系统应用完成。

5. 第三方 SDK、共享与对外提供

群星计划（应用互推）：“设置”中的“群星计划”是独立开发者之间的等权交叉推广， 用于向你展示其他鸿蒙应用，本应用也会出现在其他参与者的列表中。它不是广告 SDK：本应用未集成其代码， 不展示横幅、插屏或信息流广告，也不做个性化投放或跨应用追踪。相关请求由本应用直接向 appunion.chiyoushu.com 发起，且仅在你主动点开该入口之后发生；进入“设置”页本身不会发起群星计划请求。 发送的内容只有三项：一个由本应用在你的设备上随机生成并仅保存在本机的标识（UUID，用于统计去重， 不是设备标识符，不与你的账号、手机号或华为账号关联，清除应用数据或卸载即失效）、平台标识 harmonyos，以及被展示或被点击的那个应用的编号。我们不会发送你的游戏进度、统计、 购买记录、设备标识符或任何可识别你的信息。该服务由其运营方按其自身规则处理上述信息。 若你不点开“群星计划”，本应用不会因此功能连接网络。

本应用不集成广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。可选购买使用 HarmonyOS 系统 IAP Kit，由华为应用市场处理账号、订单、支付和续费；华为依据其支付界面及隐私政策处理相关信息。我们不会取得你的银行卡资料或账号密码； 不出售、出租个人信息，也不进行跨应用追踪或个性化广告。主动加入排行榜后，榜单会展示下文列明的名字及成绩信息。

本应用调用 HarmonyOS 提供的应用存储、振动、备份恢复和外部链接等系统能力。华为及设备系统运营方可能依据你的 设备设置和其自身政策处理应用分发、系统安全、备份恢复或故障诊断所需的信息；该等平台处理不由本应用控制。 你可在设备设置及华为相关服务中管理这些选项。

可选炼狱排行榜

排行榜可以直接浏览。只有您主动点击“加入”，应用才会使用华为账号服务请求身份凭证。未登录时由华为系统显示登录界面，您可以取消。我们不获取华为账号密码、手机号或支付密码。

我们的云函数验证身份凭证，并将账号标识通过项目专属密钥派生成稳定的玩家编号，用于让同一华为账号在本应用内保留排行榜身份。身份凭证用于本次验证，不写入排行榜数据库。排行榜数据库保存派生玩家编号、随机词组名字的编号、成绩、到达层数、最高连击、无失误标记、设备系统设置提供的两位地区码、榜单所属周及更新时间。我们不读取精确位置，也不上传游戏棋盘或笔记。

排行榜使用华为 Account Kit、AppGallery Connect 云函数与云数据库，数据处理位置为中国。其他玩家可查看排行榜上展示的名字、名次、分数、层数、连击、地区和无失误标记。名字由应用提供的固定词表组合，不支持自由文字。

加入时已有的最佳成绩只补交总榜；加入后的成绩在过层或结束时提交到总榜及当周榜。应用在本机保存玩家编号、名字编号和周榜缓存。重置游戏进度不会自动删除已上传的排行榜记录。清除应用数据或卸载后，在您再次主动加入前本应用不再上传排行榜成绩；这也不会自动删除已经上传的记录。

周榜保留约八周，过期记录在后续成绩提交触发的清理中分批删除；因此删除时间可能晚于八周。总榜记录和名字在功能运营期间保存。如需查询、更正或删除已上传记录，请联系 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com，并提供榜上显示的名字以便核实。请勿发送账号密码、身份凭证或支付信息。

华为云空间同步

在支持的设备上，会员可在系统华为云空间中启用本应用同步。此时进度、每日挑战、统计及偏好通过华为端云同步服务在同一华为账号设备间同步；排行榜身份缓存独立保存在本机，不随游戏进度同步。关闭系统同步后不再同步新进度，云端副本由华为云空间设置管理。开发者不能读取您个人云空间中的进度。

6. 存储地点、安全与保存期限

游戏数据和偏好保存在你的 HarmonyOS 设备上的应用私有目录。HarmonyOS 的沙箱和访问控制用于防止其他普通应用直接读取。 若你在系统中启用了应用备份，系统可能按照你的备份设置备份和恢复本应用数据；我们无法读取该系统备份。

重置进度仅删除游戏记录，不删除会员状态或取消订阅。会员缓存保留至清除应用数据或卸载；你可在“设置 › 管理自动续费”管理订阅，在“恢复购买”重新查询权益。其他本机数据保留至重置相应进度、清除应用数据或卸载应用。系统备份副本的保存期限由你的华为账号、 设备设置及相应系统服务规则决定，你可通过系统提供的备份管理功能处理。

7. 你的选择与权利

  • 在“设置 › 关于”中随时重新查看本政策和用户协议。
  • 在“设置 › 数据 › 重置进度”删除关卡进度、统计、脑力档案及地狱模式记录。
  • 在“设置 › 数据 › 撤回隐私同意”撤回同意；应用会退出，下次启动时再次征求你的选择。撤回不影响此前处理的合法性，也不会自动删除本机游戏数据。
  • 通过系统清除应用数据或卸载本应用，删除应用私有目录中的全部本机数据。
  • 不使用“群星计划”即可避免该功能的联网行为；清除应用数据或卸载会一并清除该功能使用的本机随机标识。
  • 通过上方邮箱提出查阅、复制、更正、删除、解释说明或投诉请求。如涉及排行榜记录，请提供榜上显示的名字以便核实；请勿发送密码、身份凭证或支付信息。

8. 未成年人保护

本应用不以收集个人信息为目的，也不会为儿童或其他用户建立广告画像。不满 14 周岁的未成年人应在父母或其他监护人 指导下阅读本政策和使用本应用；如未来功能需要处理儿童个人信息，我们会在处理前另行告知并依法取得监护人同意。

9. 数据出境

排行榜使用位于中国的华为云服务。第 5 节所述的“群星计划”请求不包含可识别你的个人信息， 仅包含本机随机标识、平台标识和被展示应用的编号。 通过系统浏览器访问本页面时，GitHub Pages 可能依据其服务规则处理网络连接所必需的信息； 该访问发生在系统浏览器中，你也可以在首次同意后离线使用主要游戏功能。

10. 政策更新

若本应用的数据处理方式、权限或第三方 SDK 发生变化，我们会先更新本政策及应用市场隐私声明。 对你的权益有重大影响或需要另行同意的变更，将通过应用内醒目方式告知并在处理前重新征得同意； 不会仅以继续使用视为同意新增的个人信息处理。

Privacy Policy (HarmonyOS)

YIGE Sliding Puzzle · Effective and last updated September 28, 2026

Summary:Core gameplay works offline without an app account. Optional purchases, Huawei Cloud sync, leaderboard and App Union use the network. Leaderboard identifiers, random-name indices and scores are uploaded only after you choose Join. No advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDK is included.

1. Controller and contact

This edition of YIGE Sliding Puzzle (“the App”) is developed and operated by Lu Liang (developer brand: Gentleflow), the controller for the practices described here. Contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com. We normally respond within 15 business days.

2. Scope

This policy applies only to the HarmonyOS App with bundle name tech.gentleflow.huarong. It does not apply to other platforms or apps.

3. Data handled by the App

Core gameplay requires no app registration. Networking supports optional Huawei purchases, leaderboard, Huawei Cloud sync and App Union as described below. AppGallery may require a HUAWEI ID for purchases, restoration, or subscription management. We do not receive account passwords or payment credentials, or read device identifiers, contacts, photos, precise location, clipboard, camera, or microphone data.

The App locally stores game progress and statistics, unfinished puzzles, preferences, and the accepted-policy version. This information is used only to save games, show statistics, restore play, apply your settings, and remember your privacy choice. It stays in private local storage except for the optional cloud synchronization and leaderboard described below.

To verify and restore membership, the App queries products, orders, and subscription status through Huawei IAP Kit and verifies signed receipts on the device. Product ID, purchase time, expiration, verification time, and membership status are cached locally. Order tokens are used to acknowledge delivery to Huawei; they are not uploaded to Gentleflow.

4. Permission and system capabilities

  • Vibration: ohos.permission.VIBRATE provides haptic feedback according to your in-App setting.
  • Network (ohos.permission.INTERNET, ohos.permission.GET_NETWORK_INFO): used for Huawei purchases, optional leaderboard and Huawei Cloud sync, and the "App Union" cross-promotion in section 5 — fetching the recommendation list, icons and details after you open that entry, and reporting impressions and clicks. The game itself (levels, daily challenge, inferno, statistics and settings) is fully offline and plays without a network. Legal and contact links are handed to your system browser or mail app, which handles any later connection.

5. SDKs and disclosure

App Union (cross-promotion): the "App Union" entry in Settings is an equal-footing cross-promotion among independent developers: it shows you other HarmonyOS apps, and this App likewise appears in other participants' lists. It is not an advertising SDK — no third-party code is embedded, no banner, interstitial or feed ads are shown, and there is no personalized targeting or cross-app tracking. Requests go directly from the App to appunion.chiyoushu.com, and only after you open that entry; opening the Settings page itself triggers no App Union request. Only three things are sent: a random identifier generated by the App on your device and stored only there (a UUID used to de-duplicate counting — not a device identifier, not linked to your account, phone number or HUAWEI ID, and void once App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled), the platform tag harmonyos, and the ID of the app being shown or tapped. We do not send your progress, statistics, purchases, device identifiers, or anything that identifies you. That service processes the above under its own operator's rules. If you do not open "App Union", the App makes no network connection for this feature.

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. Optional purchases use the system IAP Kit: AppGallery handles accounts, orders, payment, and renewals under the notices and privacy policies shown by Huawei. We do not receive your bank details or account password, sell personal data, or use it for cross-app tracking or personalized advertising.

The App uses HarmonyOS system storage, vibration, backup/restore, and external-link capabilities. Huawei or the device system operator may process information needed for distribution, security, backup, or diagnostics under your settings and their own policies. That platform processing is outside the App's control.

Optional Hell leaderboard

You can browse the leaderboard without joining. Only after you select Join does the app request an identity token through HUAWEI Account Kit. Huawei may show its sign-in screen if needed; you can cancel. We do not receive your HUAWEI ID password, phone number, or payment password.

Our cloud function verifies the token and derives a stable player identifier from the account identifier using a key specific to this app. The token is used for that verification and is not stored in the leaderboard database. Stored leaderboard data includes the derived player identifier, a name represented by fixed word-list indices, score, floor reached, best combo, flawless flag, the two-letter region code from system settings, board week, and update time. We do not access precise location or upload puzzle boards or notes for this feature.

The feature uses Huawei Account Kit and AppGallery Connect Cloud Functions and Cloud DB, with processing in China. Other players can see the displayed name, rank, score, floor, combo, region, and flawless marker. Names are combinations from fixed word lists; free text is not accepted.

When you join, your existing best score is submitted only to the all-time board. Subsequent scores are submitted to both the current-week and all-time boards at stage completion or the end of a run. The app stores your player identifier, name indices, and weekly cache locally. Resetting game progress does not delete uploaded leaderboard records. After clearing app data or uninstalling, leaderboard uploads stop until you explicitly join again; existing server records are not automatically deleted.

Weekly records are retained for approximately eight weeks and then removed in batches when later score submissions trigger cleanup, so deletion may take longer. All-time records and names remain while the feature operates. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with the name shown on the leaderboard so we can verify the request. Do not send passwords, identity tokens, or payment details.

Huawei Cloud sync

On supported devices, Premium members can enable app synchronization in Huawei Cloud settings. Progress, daily records, statistics and preferences then synchronize between devices using the same HUAWEI ID through Huawei cloud synchronization services. Leaderboard identity caches stay local and are separate from game-progress synchronization. Turning synchronization off stops new progress syncing; manage cloud copies in Huawei Cloud settings. The developer cannot read progress in your personal Huawei Cloud space.

6. Storage, security, and retention

App data is protected by HarmonyOS app-private storage and sandbox controls. If system app backup is enabled, HarmonyOS may back up and restore this data under your system settings; we cannot read that backup. Reset progress deletes gameplay records but does not delete membership or cancel subscriptions. Membership is cached until App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Use Settings › Manage automatic renewal to manage subscriptions, or Restore purchases to refresh access. Other local data remains until you reset it, clear App data, or uninstall the App. System backup retention is controlled through your device and Huawei services.

7. Your choices

  • Review these documents anytime under Settings › About.
  • Use Settings › Data › Reset progress to delete progress and gameplay statistics.
  • Use Settings › Data › Withdraw privacy consent. The App closes and asks again at the next launch; withdrawal does not automatically delete local game data.
  • Clear App data or uninstall to remove the App's private on-device data.
  • Not using "App Union" avoids networking for that feature; clearing App data or uninstalling also clears the local random identifier it uses.
  • Email us to request access, correction, deletion, explanation, or to complain. For leaderboard records, provide the displayed name so we can verify the request. Do not send passwords, identity tokens or payment information.

8. Children

The App does not build advertising profiles or seek children's personal information. Children under 14 should use it with a parent or guardian. We will provide a new notice and obtain legally required guardian consent before any future processing of children's data.

9. International transfers

The leaderboard uses Huawei cloud services located in China. The "App Union" requests described in section 5 contain no information that identifies you — only a local random identifier, the platform tag, and the ID of the app being shown. If you open this page in a browser, GitHub Pages may process connection data under its own terms. The principal game can be used offline after the first consent.

10. Updates

We will update this policy and the AppGallery privacy declaration before changing data practices, permissions, or third-party SDKs. Material changes will be prominently disclosed, and fresh consent will be requested before processing where required.
