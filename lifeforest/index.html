Common questions / 常见问题

How do I restore a subscription? / 如何恢复订阅？

Open the LifeForest paywall and tap “Restore Purchases,” using the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.
打开人生森林会员页，点击“恢复购买”，并使用购买时的同一 Apple 账户。

How do I manage or cancel it? / 如何管理或取消？

Go to iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › LifeForest. Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.
前往 iOS 设置 ›〔你的名字〕› 订阅 › 人生森林。删除应用不会取消仍在生效的订阅。

Where is my forest data stored? / 森林数据保存在哪里？

Your profile, trees, plans, and practice history are stored locally in the App's private shared container so the LifeForest widget can display them. LifeForest does not currently provide account or iCloud sync.
你的档案、树、计划和练习记录保存在本机应用私有共享容器中，供人生森林小组件展示。目前不提供账号或 iCloud 同步。

What is sent to AI? / 哪些内容会发送给 AI？

Only when an AI feature runs, LifeForest sends the context needed for that feature, which may include goals, planning answers, tree and node details, practice rhythm, recent check-in text, minutes, and mood. Avoid entering confidential or identifying information.
仅在使用 AI 功能时，人生森林会发送该功能所需的上下文，可能包括目标、规划回答、树和节点信息、练习节奏、近期打卡文字、时长和心情。请勿输入机密或可识别个人身份的信息。

How do I remove local data? / 如何删除本机数据？

Use the in-app “Start Over” option where available, or delete LifeForest and its widget from all devices. Subscription records remain with Apple and RevenueCat under their policies.
可使用应用内“重新开始”功能（如当前版本提供），或从所有设备删除人生森林及其小组件。订阅记录仍会由 Apple 与 RevenueCat 按各自政策保存。
