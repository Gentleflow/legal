Privacy Policy

LifeForest · Effective July 20, 2026

In short: LifeForest needs no account, has no ads or third-party analytics, and does not track you across apps. Your forest is stored locally. When you choose an AI feature, the relevant goal and practice context is sent to an AI service to generate the result. Apple and RevenueCat process subscriptions.

1. Who we are

LifeForest (“the App”) is developed by Gentleflow (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains the App's data practices. Contact gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com with privacy questions.

2. Information the App handles

  • Local profile and practice data: nickname, goals, planning answers, skill trees, nodes, routines, check-ins, notes, mood selections, practice duration, streaks, settings, reminder preferences, and feature-usage counters.
  • AI inputs and outputs: when you use an AI planning, coaching, reaction, suggestion, adjustment, or checkpoint feature, the App sends the context needed to answer. Depending on the feature, that can include goals, planning answers, tree names and reasons, node details, practice rhythm, completion standards, recent check-in text, dates, minutes, mood, and derived progress. AI-generated responses return to the App and may become part of your local forest data.
  • Subscription data: product, transaction, entitlement, country/region, and device/OS-related information handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.
  • Technical data: when an online AI or subscription service is contacted, its provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as IP address, timestamp, device/OS information, request status, and usage counts.
  • Photos and shares: if you tap Save, the App requests add-only photo-library permission and writes the generated forest card you selected. If you tap Share, Apple's share sheet sends the card only to the destination you choose. The App does not read your photo library.
  • Notifications and widgets: reminders are scheduled locally after permission is granted. Forest data is shared locally with the LifeForest widget through an Apple App Group container.

3. How and why we use information

We use this information to save and display your forest, personalize practice paths and reminders, generate AI-assisted content you request, provide widgets and sharing, verify subscription access, restore purchases, prevent service abuse, troubleshoot failures, and meet legal obligations. We do not use IDFA, serve ads, sell personal information, or track activity across other companies' apps or websites.

4. Storage and service providers

  • On-device storage. Your forest data is encoded in the App's private local/App Group storage. LifeForest currently has no Gentleflow account or iCloud-sync service.
  • OminiGate and upstream AI providers. AI requests pass through OminiGate to the selected model provider. OminiGate states that prompt and response bodies are not stored by default, while usage metadata and brief operational logs may be processed. Its upstream providers may also process requests. See OminiGate's Privacy Policy. Please do not include secrets, government identifiers, financial account data, medical records, or other information you do not want processed by an AI service.
  • Apple. Apple processes App Store payments, subscription records, system notifications, photo-library writes, sharing, and optional App Store diagnostics under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat. RevenueCat verifies and restores purchases and manages entitlements. It may process a pseudonymous App User ID plus transaction, product, country, device, OS, IP, and service-event information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Diagnostics

If “Share With App Developers” is enabled on your device, Apple may provide us with crash and performance reports through App Store Connect. Such reports may include technical diagnostics Apple makes available under your settings. LifeForest contains no third-party analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you reset it in the App or delete the App and its related local container. Temporary share images may remain in system temporary storage until iOS removes them. AI providers, Apple, and RevenueCat retain their records according to their own policies and legal obligations. To request deletion of data, if any, that Gentleflow can reasonably associate with you, email us. We may need purchase or request details to locate it; some transaction records cannot be deleted where law requires retention.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Do not use an AI feature if you do not want its displayed context sent to an AI service.
  • Change notification or photo permission in iOS Settings.
  • Use privacy controls before sharing a forest card, and choose its destination through Apple's share sheet.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Reset local progress in the App where available, or delete the App.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise an applicable right.

8. Children

LifeForest does not ask users to create an account and is not designed to solicit a child's contact details. AI text fields are free-form, however. A child who cannot legally consent to online data processing in their location should use AI features only with a parent or guardian's permission and should not enter identifying or sensitive personal information. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us so we can evaluate the request with our providers.

9. Security and international processing

We use platform protections and encrypted HTTPS connections, but no system is completely secure. Online service providers may process data in countries other than yours under their safeguards and applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the App or its providers change. Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

Email gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com.

隐私政策

人生森林 · 2026 年 7 月 20 日生效

一句话：人生森林无需注册账号，无广告、无第三方分析，也不跨应用追踪。你的森林数据保存在本机；当你主动使用 AI 功能时，与该功能有关的目标和练习上下文会发送给 AI 服务以生成结果；订阅由 Apple 与 RevenueCat 处理。

1. 我们是谁

人生森林（“本应用”）由 Gentleflow（“我们”）开发。本政策说明本应用的数据处理方式。如有隐私问题，请联系 gentleflowoverseas@gmail.com。

2. 本应用处理的信息

  • 本机档案与练习数据：昵称、目标、规划回答、技能树、节点、练习节奏、打卡、笔记、心情选择、练习时长、连续天数、设置、提醒偏好与功能使用次数。
  • AI 输入与输出：当你使用 AI 规划、教练、即时回应、目标推荐、路径调整或检查点拆解时，本应用会发送生成回答所需的上下文。视功能而定，可能包括目标、规划回答、树名与种树理由、节点详情、练习节奏、完成标准、近期打卡文字、日期、分钟数、心情与推导出的进度。AI 返回内容会显示在应用中，并可能成为本机森林数据的一部分。
  • 订阅数据：Apple 与 RevenueCat 处理的商品、交易、权益、国家/地区及设备/系统相关信息。我们不会收到你的银行卡详情或 Apple 账户凭据。
  • 技术数据：当应用连接在线 AI 或订阅服务时，服务商可能收到常规请求元数据，例如 IP 地址、时间、设备/系统信息、请求状态与用量。
  • 照片与分享：点击“保存”后，应用会请求“仅添加照片”权限，并写入你选择的森林纪念卡；点击“分享”后，只有你通过 Apple 分享面板选择的目标会收到该图片。本应用不会读取你的照片图库。
  • 通知与小组件：取得权限后，提醒仅在本机安排。人生森林通过 Apple App Group 私有容器在本机与小组件共享森林数据。

3. 使用目的

我们处理上述信息，用于保存和展示森林、个性化练习路径与提醒、生成你请求的 AI 内容、提供小组件与分享、验证订阅权益、恢复购买、防止服务滥用、排查故障及履行法律义务。我们不使用 IDFA、不投放广告、不出售个人信息，也不跨其他公司的应用或网站追踪你。

4. 存储与服务商

  • 本机存储。森林数据编码保存在应用私有的本机/App Group 存储中。人生森林目前不提供 Gentleflow 账号或 iCloud 同步服务。
  • OminiGate 与上游 AI 服务商。AI 请求经 OminiGate 转发给所选模型服务商。OminiGate 声明默认不保存提示词和模型回复正文，但可能处理用量元数据和短期运行日志；其上游服务商也可能处理请求。详见 OminiGate 隐私政策。请勿输入密码、政府证件号码、金融账户信息、病历或其他不希望由 AI 服务处理的信息。
  • Apple。Apple 依据其隐私政策处理 App Store 支付、订阅记录、系统通知、照片写入、分享及可选的 App Store 诊断。
  • RevenueCat。RevenueCat 用于校验和恢复购买、管理会员权益，可能处理化名 App User ID，以及交易、商品、国家/地区、设备、系统、IP 与服务事件信息。详见 RevenueCat 隐私政策。

5. 诊断

若你在设备中开启“与 App 开发者共享”，Apple 可能通过 App Store Connect 向我们提供崩溃和性能报告；报告可能含 Apple 依照你的设置提供的技术诊断。本应用不含第三方分析 SDK 或广告 SDK。

6. 留存与删除

本机数据会保留至你在应用内重置，或删除应用及其关联本机容器。临时分享图片可能留在系统临时目录，直至 iOS 清理。AI 服务商、Apple 与 RevenueCat 按各自政策和法律义务留存记录。如需删除 Gentleflow 能合理关联到你的数据（如有），请发送邮件；我们可能需要购买或请求信息来定位，法律要求保存的部分交易记录可能无法删除。

7. 你的选择与权利

  • 如不希望相关上下文发送给 AI 服务，请勿使用对应 AI 功能。
  • 可在 iOS 设置中调整通知或照片权限。
  • 分享森林卡片前可使用隐私开关，并通过 Apple 分享面板自行选择接收方。
  • 可在 iOS 设置 ›〔你的名字〕› 订阅 中管理或取消订阅。
  • 可使用应用内重置功能（如当前版本提供）清除本机进度，或删除应用。
  • 根据你所在地法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制或反对处理，以及向数据保护机构投诉的权利。可联系我们行使适用权利。

8. 儿童隐私

人生森林无需注册账号，也不会主动索取儿童联系方式，但 AI 输入框允许自由输入。未达到所在地网络数据处理同意年龄的儿童，应在父母或监护人许可下使用 AI 功能，且不要输入可识别身份或敏感个人信息。如你认为儿童提交了个人信息，请联系我们，以便我们与服务商核实处理。

9. 安全与跨境处理

我们使用系统平台保护及加密 HTTPS 连接，但任何系统都无法保证绝对安全。在线服务商可能在你所在国家/地区以外处理数据，并受其保障措施与适用法律约束。

10. 政策变更

随着应用或服务商变化，我们可能更新本政策。重大变更会在此发布，并更新生效日期。
